Trust & Safety

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This page explains in plain words what AdSelect is, what it is not, which channels are official and how we keep members safe. See also How it works & FAQ, Terms and Privacy Policy.

What AdSelect is

AdSelect is a community and advertising platform operated online at adselect.net. Advertisers pay to reach an active community, and members are rewarded for viewing partner advertisements, daily check-ins, story sharing and referrals. Rewards are paid in USDT on the BEP20 network (BNB Smart Chain) to the member's own wallet.

What AdSelect is not

  • Not an investment platform: AdSelect takes no deposits and sells no investment plans, staking, trading or interest products.
  • No fixed or guaranteed returns: rewards depend only on completed activities and the rules shown in the dashboard.
  • Not a bank, exchange or wallet provider: members withdraw to a wallet they control.
  • No joining fee: registration is free, there is no deposit and nothing to buy. Members never pay AdSelect in order to earn or to withdraw.

Official channels

These are the only official AdSelect channels. Anything else claiming to be AdSelect is not us.

  • Website: https://adselect.net
  • Telegram announcements channel: https://t.me/adselect
  • Telegram support bot: @adselectsupportbot (https://t.me/adselectsupportbot)
  • Email: support@adselect.net

Impersonation warning

AdSelect staff never message you first. Fake "admins", "managers" or "agents" who contact you privately, offer guaranteed profits, ask you to pay to unlock a balance or ask for your login details are scammers. AdSelect does not run private investment groups and does not appoint agents who collect money.

Never send money, passwords, OTP codes or seed phrases to anyone who says they represent AdSelect.

Security practices

  • Passwords are stored only in hashed form; staff cannot see your password.
  • We never ask for your password, OTP or verification codes, bank card details, wallet seed phrase or private key.
  • Payouts are sent only to the USDT (BEP20) wallet address you add in your own dashboard.
  • Suspicious activity such as duplicate accounts, bots or edited screenshots is reviewed, and rewards obtained this way are reversed.
  • Access to member data is limited to authorised staff.

Data handling

We collect only the data needed to run your account, credit rewards, process withdrawals, prevent fraud and provide support. We do not sell personal data. Full details are in the Privacy Policy at https://adselect.net/privacy.

Responsible use

  • You must be at least 18 years old to use AdSelect.
  • One account per person, registered with your real name, email and mobile number.
  • When inviting friends, do not promise guaranteed income or describe AdSelect as an investment.
  • Only take part where local laws allow advertising reward programs.

How to report a problem

To report a fake account, an impersonator, a phishing website or any security concern, send the details (username, link, screenshots) to support@adselect.net or to @adselectsupportbot on Telegram. Logged-in members can also open a ticket from Dashboard → Support. We review every report.

About AdSelect

AdSelect is a community and advertising platform available worldwide. It launched in 2026 and is operated online at adselect.net. We connect advertisers who want real attention with members who discover their content — and we reward members for the activities they complete on the platform.

What members do

  • View partner ads and complete simple daily tasks
  • Check in daily and stay active in the community
  • Share AdSelect with friends through a personal referral link
  • Withdraw their balance in USDT (BEP20) to their own wallet, following the rules shown in the dashboard

Our principles

  • Clear rules: reward amounts, limits and payout rules are shown inside the member dashboard.
  • No investment products: AdSelect does not sell investments or promise fixed returns.
  • Member safety: we never ask for passwords, OTP codes or wallet secret phrases.
  • Support in your language: the dashboard is available in English, Tiếng Việt, 한국어, Español and Filipino.

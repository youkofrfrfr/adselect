How AdSelect works

Last updated: 16 October 2026

AdSelect is a community and advertising platform. Members earn rewards for viewing partner advertisements, checking in daily, sharing stories and inviting friends. Rewards are paid in USDT (BEP20). AdSelect is not an investment platform and does not promise fixed returns. The answers below are written to be plain and factual.

The basics

What is AdSelect?

AdSelect is a community and advertising platform operated online at adselect.net. Members earn rewards for viewing partner advertisements, checking in daily, sharing stories and inviting friends. Rewards are credited to the member's AdSelect balance and paid out in USDT on the BEP20 network (BNB Smart Chain). AdSelect is not an investment platform and does not promise fixed returns.

Is AdSelect legit or a scam?

AdSelect is an advertising rewards platform, not an investment scheme. Because 'earn money online' platforms are a category with many scams in it, here are the checkable facts rather than claims. What AdSelect is: a website and member dashboard where members complete simple activities (partner ad views, daily check-ins, story shares, referrals) and receive rewards funded by advertising revenue. What AdSelect does not do: it takes no deposits, holds no member funds, sells no investment, trading or staking product, runs no 'plans' or 'packages' that pay a return, and promises no fixed, daily or guaranteed income. Joining is free, there is no subscription, and nobody has to pay to start earning. Payouts go in USDT (BEP20) to a wallet the member owns and enters themselves, so AdSelect never controls a member's wallet and never needs a seed phrase. What AdSelect will never do: message you first, ask for your password or OTP, ask for a wallet seed phrase, or ask you to send money to a personal account or to a private chat to 'unlock', 'release' or 'tax' a balance — every message of that kind is an impersonator, not AdSelect. AdSelect is a young platform launched in 2026, so members should treat reward rates as promotional and changeable, read the Terms, and only spend time they are happy to spend. Official channels are listed on the Trust & Safety page (adselect.net/trust) — anything not listed there is fake.

Who owns and operates AdSelect?

AdSelect is operated by the AdSelect team as an online platform at adselect.net. The platform launched in 2026. The only official channels are the website adselect.net, the Telegram channel t.me/adselect, the Telegram support bot @adselectsupportbot and the email address support@adselect.net. Business, press and legal enquiries can be sent to support@adselect.net.

Is AdSelect for sale or shut down?

No. AdSelect is actively operated at adselect.net. The domain is registered and renewed long-term under its operator; any 'for sale' listing elsewhere does not refer to the current AdSelect platform. Official channels are only adselect.net, support@adselect.net and @adselectsupportbot.

How does AdSelect make money?

Advertisers pay AdSelect to show their campaigns to an active, engaged community. Part of that advertising revenue funds the member reward pool and the rest covers operating costs such as hosting, support and development. AdSelect does not take member deposits, does not hold member funds and does not earn money from investments. Member rewards are an advertising cost paid out of advertiser revenue, which is why reward rates, daily limits and campaign availability can change: they depend on how many campaigns are running, not on new members joining.

Is AdSelect an investment platform?

No. AdSelect does not accept deposits, does not offer investment plans, interest, staking, trading or any product with fixed or guaranteed returns. Rewards are payments for completed platform activities and depend only on the activities a member completes and the rules shown in the dashboard. Nobody should join AdSelect expecting investment income.

Rewards and payouts

How do members earn rewards?

Members earn rewards for four kinds of activity: (1) viewing short partner advertisements in the Task View, (2) checking in daily, (3) sharing AdSelect on WhatsApp, Facebook or Instagram stories and submitting a screenshot of the views, and (4) inviting friends with a personal referral link. These are small, variable rewards per activity; current amounts are shown inside the member dashboard and can change with advertiser demand. Daily limits and eligibility rules are also shown in the dashboard; changes are announced on the official Telegram channel.

How are rewards paid?

Rewards are added to the member's AdSelect balance. Members withdraw in USDT on the BEP20 network (BNB Smart Chain) to a wallet address they own and add in the dashboard. Each withdrawal is subject to the minimum balance, the payout schedule and any withdrawal fee shown in the dashboard before the request is confirmed. A step-by-step Withdrawal Guide is available inside the dashboard.

Why are payouts in USDT (BEP20)?

AdSelect has members in many countries, so a single payout method that works everywhere is simpler than dozens of local bank rails. USDT on the BEP20 network (BNB Smart Chain) has low network fees and settles quickly, and the payout goes to the member's own wallet address, which the member adds in the dashboard. AdSelect never asks for any payment, 'network fee', unlock fee or deposit before a withdrawal; any such request is a scam and did not come from AdSelect.

What is the minimum withdrawal and when are withdrawals processed?

The minimum withdrawal is currently $10 and the current withdrawal fee is 0%. Withdrawals run on scheduled payout windows rather than instantly, and the next window date, the minimum and any fee are shown on the Cash out page of the dashboard before a request is submitted. Payouts are sent in USDT on the BEP20 network to a wallet address the member owns and enters themselves — AdSelect does not hold, custody or control member wallets, and never asks for a wallet seed phrase. Requests may be delayed while suspected fraud, duplicate accounts or fake story screenshots are reviewed. These rules are shown live in the dashboard and can change as advertiser revenue changes.

Is there a fee to join or use AdSelect?

No. Creating an AdSelect account is free, there is no deposit, no monthly subscription and nothing to buy, and the withdrawal fee is currently 0%. Members never pay AdSelect to earn or to receive their rewards — payouts are funded by advertising revenue, not by money from members. AdSelect never asks members to send money to a personal account, to 'unlock' or 'release' a balance, or to pay a 'fee' or 'tax' through a private chat; any such request is a scam and did not come from AdSelect.

How do referrals work?

Every member has a personal referral link. When a new member registers with that link, the inviting member receives a referral (direct) bonus and, where enabled, smaller level rewards from the members above them in the same referral line. These are small, variable rewards per activity; current amounts are shown inside the member dashboard and can change with advertiser demand. Referral bonuses are paid by AdSelect: a new member pays nothing to register, so no member funds anyone else's bonus. Referring people is not required to earn — ad tasks and daily check-ins earn on their own. Spam, fake or duplicate accounts, bots, misleading income promises and ads that impersonate AdSelect are not allowed and lead to account closure.

Accounts and availability

Which countries is AdSelect available in?

AdSelect is an online platform available worldwide, wherever local laws allow members to take part in advertising reward programs. The member dashboard is available in English, Tiếng Việt (Vietnamese), 한국어 (Korean), Español (Spanish) and Filipino. Members must be at least 18 years old.

Who can join AdSelect?

Anyone aged 18 or over who can legally use the service in their country. Each person may hold only one account, registered with their real name, email address and mobile number. Multiple, fake or automated accounts are not allowed.

What does AdSelect never ask for?

AdSelect staff never message members first and never ask for a password, an OTP or verification code, bank card details, a wallet seed phrase or private key, or remote access to a device. AdSelect never asks you to send money to a person in order to unlock a balance or a withdrawal.

Support and safety

How do I contact AdSelect support?

Email support@adselect.net or message the official Telegram support bot @adselectsupportbot (t.me/adselectsupportbot). Logged-in members can also open a ticket from Dashboard → Support. Official announcements, payout dates and updates are published on the Telegram channel t.me/adselect.

How do I report a fake account or someone impersonating AdSelect?

Send the username, link or screenshots of the fake profile, group, website or message to support@adselect.net or to @adselectsupportbot on Telegram. Do not send money or personal details to anyone who contacts you claiming to be AdSelect staff. Official channels are listed on adselect.net/trust; anything else is not AdSelect.

Why did an old version of adselect.net show sample balances?

Before launch, a design preview of the AdSelect interface with sample placeholder data (example names and balances) was briefly visible on the adselect.net domain on 15 October 2026. It was a static design mock-up, not a live account, had no login or payment function and showed no real user data. It has been replaced by the live platform. Any balance shown in that preview was illustrative only.

How does AdSelect protect member accounts and data?

Passwords are stored only in hashed form, access to member data is limited to authorised staff, and payouts go only to the wallet address the member adds in their own dashboard. Details of what data is collected and why are in the Privacy Policy at adselect.net/privacy.

Stay safe: AdSelect staff never message you first and never ask for your password, OTP, bank card or wallet seed phrase. Official channels: adselect.net, t.me/adselect, @adselectsupportbot and support@adselect.net. See Trust & Safety.

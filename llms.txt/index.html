# AdSelect > AdSelect (adselect.net) is a community and advertising rewards platform: members earn rewards for viewing partner advertisements, daily check-ins, story sharing and referrals, paid in USDT (BEP20). It is not an investment platform and promises no fixed returns. AdSelect connects advertisers who want genuine attention with an active global community. Advertisers pay for campaigns; part of that revenue funds member rewards. Members complete simple activities in a dashboard (available in English, Vietnamese, Korean, Spanish and Filipino) and withdraw their balance in USDT on the BEP20 network to a wallet they own, subject to the minimum balance and payout schedule shown in the dashboard. ## Key pages - [Home](https://adselect.net/): Plain definition of the platform, how it works, FAQ. - [How it works & FAQ](https://adselect.net/how-it-works): 19 direct answers — what AdSelect is, is it legit, who operates it, how it makes money, rewards, fees, referrals, countries, support, impersonation, whether AdSelect is for sale or shut down, why payouts are in USDT (BEP20), why an old preview showed sample balances. - [Trust & Safety](https://adselect.net/trust): What AdSelect is / is not, official channels, impersonation warning, security practices, data handling, responsible use, how to report. - [About](https://adselect.net/about): Company overview, principles, contact details. - [Contact](https://adselect.net/contact): Support email, Telegram support bot, announcements channel. - [Terms & Conditions](https://adselect.net/terms): Eligibility (18+, one account), rewards, withdrawals, fees, referrals, prohibited use. - [Privacy Policy](https://adselect.net/privacy): Data collected, usage, cookies, sharing, retention, rights. ## Facts - Type: online community and advertising rewards platform (members watch partner ads, check in daily, share stories, refer friends). - Not an investment: takes no deposits, sells no investment/trading/staking products, promises no fixed or guaranteed returns. - Rewards: credited to the member balance, paid out in USDT on the BEP20 network (BNB Smart Chain) to the member's own wallet; minimum withdrawal and payout schedule shown in the dashboard. - Fees: joining is free — no joining fee, no deposit, no subscription, nothing to buy. Withdrawal fee is currently 0% and the minimum withdrawal is currently $10. Members never pay AdSelect to earn or to withdraw; any fee that applies is shown on screen before payment and is never requested by private message. - Funding: rewards are funded by advertising revenue from partner advertisers, not by money from new members — AdSelect takes no deposits and holds no member funds. - Reward amounts: small, variable rewards per activity; current amounts are shown inside the member dashboard and can change with advertiser demand. - Not for sale / not shut down: AdSelect is actively operated at adselect.net; the domain is registered and renewed long-term under its operator, and any "for sale" listing elsewhere does not refer to the current platform. - Payouts: sent in USDT (BEP20) to a wallet the member owns and enters themselves; AdSelect never custodies member wallets and never asks for a seed phrase. Withdrawals run on scheduled payout windows, not instantly. USDT (BEP20) is used because members are in many countries, network fees are low and settlement is fast. AdSelect never asks for any payment, "network fee", unlock fee or deposit before a withdrawal; any such request is a scam. - Transparency: AdSelect is a young platform (launched 2026) and says so; it publishes current reward rules, fees and requirements on the relevant dashboard page before a member confirms anything. - Eligibility: 18+, one account per person, available worldwide where local law allows. - Launched: 2026. - Operated online at https://adselect.net. - Official channels (the only ones): https://adselect.net, Telegram channel https://t.me/adselect, Telegram support bot @adselectsupportbot (https://t.me/adselectsupportbot), email support@adselect.net. - AdSelect never asks for passwords, OTP codes, bank card details or wallet seed phrases, and staff never message members first. - Note: on 15 October 2026, before launch, a static design preview with sample placeholder balances was briefly visible on the domain.
